Building agentic AI that survives production.

Jothsna Praveena Pendyala is an AI Platform Architect and Senior Data Scientist at Infosys, where she leads an enterprise Agentic AI platform at AT&T — production multi-agent systems on LangGraph, Azure Kubernetes Service, and custom MCP servers. Previously on Amazon's AGI team, working on the Nova models. IEEE Senior Member, Chair & Chief Editor of ACM Dallas, and a published researcher in IEEE and Springer venues.

She is the co-founder of DeepAgentLabs, a specification-first open-source ecosystem for AI operations, and of AgenticEdAcademy, an initiative for agentic AI education and skills.
