Building Reliable Agentic Platforms on Kubernetes: Beyond Pod Health

SREday Dallas Q4 · evolv consulting HQ, Dallas, TX · October 23, 2026

Healthy pods don't mean completed tasks. This talk covered the platform controls that make agentic systems reliable in production — durable checkpoints, idempotency contracts, and quota-bounded autoscaling — with three real failure scenarios: a worker terminating mid-task, model rate-limits, and a tool timing out after changing an external system.
