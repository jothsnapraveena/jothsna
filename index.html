Professional Summary

Overview

Jothsna Praveena Pendyala is an AI Platform Architect and Senior Data Scientist at Infosys, serving as the principal architect of an enterprise Agentic AI platform at AT&T — one of the largest telecommunications companies in the United States. She architects and owns a production-scale, multi-agent AI system deployed across AT&T's engineering organization, built on LangGraph, Azure Kubernetes Service, and a custom MCP server infrastructure.

Her expertise spans agentic AI systems design, Kubernetes platform engineering, LLM infrastructure, and AI observability at enterprise scale. She has prior experience on Amazon's AGI team, working directly on the Nova family of large language models. She is a published researcher with peer-reviewed work in IEEE, Springer, and other indexed venues, an editor of a forthcoming book published by Taylor & Francis, and a contributing author to four books in the field of AI and data engineering.

She is an IEEE Senior Member, holds elected and appointed leadership roles at the Association for Computing Machinery (ACM) — including Chair and Chief Editor of the ACM Dallas Chapter — and has been an invited panelist at major international technology conferences including the Linux Foundation's Open Source Summit North America 2026.
